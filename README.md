# **About US**

## **Shiro**

Write, reflect, and come home to yourself.

Journaling is one of the oldest human rituals.
Long before algorithms and endless feeds, people wrote —
to see themselves more clearly,
to listen inwardly,
to stay kindly beside themselves.

But in the modern world, we are drowning in noise.
We scroll more than we breathe,
and rarely pause long enough
to ask ourselves how we really feel.

Shiro was born to bring that pause back —
to make reflection feel alive again.

In Japanese, “Shiro” means white —
a symbol of space, clarity, and quiet.
We hope to offer everyone a clean, open canvas
to record themselves freely —
without judgment, without noise,
just the gentle truth of being.

We use the latest AI not to replace your thoughts,
but to walk beside them —
to listen, to understand, to remind you
that your story matters.

Through words, images, and voice,
Shiro helps you be seen — even by yourself.
It lowers the barrier to self-connection,
and turns journaling into a gentle dialogue
between who you are and who you’re becoming.

Because when you begin to write,
something shifts quietly inside you.
Not stronger, but softer.
Not louder, but more at peace.

Your rhythm will draw the right things to you.
And your life — slowly, deeply — will begin to change.

⸻

Shiro — a white space for your inner world to unfold.

# **Shiro Privacy Policy**

### **A gentle note before we begin**

At **FREEDOMAI PRIVATE LIMITED**, we built **Shiro** for one reason — to help people know themselves better through honest reflection.

That means your words, memories, and emotions are sacred to us. Protecting them isn’t just a legal duty; it’s part of what makes Shiro meaningful.

This policy explains how your data is handled — simply, clearly, and transparently.

If you ever want clarity or deletion, write to us anytime at **support@freedomai.fun**.

### **1. What Shiro Is (and Isn’t)**

Shiro is your private digital space — a personal journal enhanced by AI reflections, not a social platform.

Your notes, images, and voice recordings are yours alone.

They are stored safely, encrypted using **AES encryption** in our database, and can only be viewed when you sign in.

Even our developers can’t read them.

We use your content only to provide the service and generate reflections that feel personal and relevant to you.

### **2. What Information We Collect**

We collect only what’s necessary to make Shiro work:

- **Basic account data** (e.g., your email or sign-in method).
- **Your entries** — text, images, and audio recordings — all encrypted before storage.
- **Device and usage data** — technical details like OS, app version, or crash logs to keep things running smoothly.
- **Biometric access (optional)** — Face ID / Touch ID handled entirely by your device. We never see or store your biometric data.

We don’t track your location, contact list, or anything unrelated to journaling.

### **3. How We Use Your Data**

We process your data for a few core reasons:

- To save, sync, and display your entries across devices.
- To provide AI-generated reflections and summaries.
- To fix bugs, improve reliability, and make your journaling experience smoother.
- To ensure account security and prevent abuse.

When we use analytics tools (like Mixpanel or Sentry), we never send your private entries — only anonymized technical data.

### **4. How We Keep It Safe**

Security is built into every layer of Shiro:

- **Encryption:** All journal content is encrypted using **AES-256** before it ever reaches our database.
- **Transmission Security:** Every connection between your device and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS.
- **Access Control:** Only a very small number of authorized engineers can access limited system logs for maintenance — never user entries.
- **Internal Privacy Culture:** Our team is trained to treat privacy as product design, not policy paperwork.

### **5. Third-Party Partners (Who Help Us Run Shiro)**

We rely on a few trusted partners:

- **Google Cloud Firestore** — for encrypted cloud data storage.
- **OpenAI & Anthropic** — to power Shiro’s reflection features. Data sent to these models is **anonymized** and **not stored** by them.
- **Mixpanel / Sentry / LogRocket** — for app analytics and performance monitoring. Sensitive data is filtered out before sending.
- **Apple / Google / Meta advertising pixels** (optional) — used only to measure campaign reach, never tied to your personal journal data.


### **6. Data Retention & Deletion**

You can delete your account anytime in-app or by contacting us.

Once deleted, all related content — text, images, audio — will be permanently erased from our systems.

Encrypted backups are retained for up to **30 days** purely for disaster recovery, after which they’re purged automatically.

### **7. Your Rights**

You always have the right to:

- Access your data.
- Correct or update any inaccuracies.
- Request complete deletion of your account and content.

Simply email **support@freedomai.fun** and we’ll assist you promptly.

### **8. Incident Response & Transparency**

If anything ever compromises user data, we’ll inform affected users as soon as possible and take swift corrective measures.

Our internal processes are continuously improved to prevent, detect, and resolve incidents responsibly.

### **9. Policy Updates**

We may update this Privacy Policy as Shiro evolves.

If changes affect how your data is used, we’ll let you know clearly — through in-app notice or email.
